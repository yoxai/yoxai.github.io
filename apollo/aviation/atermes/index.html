How it works

Detect early. Respond only when needed.

01

SURICATE detection

An AI multi-sensor system: optronics, embedded AI and edge computing watch the airfield and identify threats in real time.

02

Bird Repelling System

Acoustic and laser repellers switch on automatically when SURICATE finds birds near runways and taxiways, and stay off otherwise, saving energy and sparing wildlife.

03

VIGGIL surveillance

One platform joining optronic sensors, radars, drones, command centres, AI analytics and secure communications, from a single site to a national network.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does the Bird Repelling System decide when to act?
SURICATE’s AI detects birds near runways and taxiways and triggers the acoustic and laser repellers automatically. The system runs only when a threat is detected, which saves energy and reduces disturbance to wildlife.
What is VIGGIL?
VIGGIL is ATERMES’ end-to-end surveillance platform for borders, military zones and critical infrastructure. It combines optronic sensors, radars, UAVs, command centres, AI analytics and secure communications.
Can ATERMES systems feed our existing control room?
Apollo integrates detection alerts and video into the airport’s existing CCTV and control-room workflows, so operators work from one screen.
