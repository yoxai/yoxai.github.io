For airlines and MROs

Operations software already flying.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What aviation systems does Apollo deliver?
Passenger processing (common-use check-in, kiosks, biometrics), passenger information displays and public address, airport management and AODB integration, border control, inflight connectivity, and airfield surveillance with AI video analytics.
Which partners does Apollo work with in aviation?
SITA for airport IT and passenger processing, and ATERMES for bird-strike prevention and perimeter surveillance. Apollo also builds its own platform, InsightX, for AI video intelligence.
Can new systems work with what our airport already runs?
Yes. Integration is the core of the work: Apollo connects new systems to existing DCS, AODB and airline systems, and brings alerts into one control room.
Does Apollo build software for airlines and MROs?
Yes. The Northern Technik Operations Platform is live with Northern Technik Pvt Ltd, and the Flight Ops Suite links live flights to maintenance work.
