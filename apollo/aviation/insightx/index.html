FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does InsightX store video and data?
On your premises. InsightX is deployed on-premise for data sovereignty, with air-gapped options, role-based access and audit logging.
Does InsightX work with existing cameras?
InsightX integrates with CCTV and control-room workflows, so most projects start with the cameras and command centre a site already has.
Who is InsightX for?
Airports, border agencies, smart-city programmes and operators of critical infrastructure.
