Smoother passenger journeys, from check-in to gate.

What it covers

The technology behind the whole passenger journey.

01

Common-use passenger processing

Check-in counters, kiosks and gates that any airline can use (CUPPS). Airports add capacity without each airline installing its own hardware.

02

Self-service and bag drop

Kiosks and self bag-drop that move queues away from counters and let staff focus on passengers who need help.

03

Biometric journeys

SITA Smart Path uses facial biometrics so passengers can move through the airport without showing documents at every step.

04

Digital borders

Border management used by more than 70 governments, with advance passenger data and automated clearance.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is CUPPS?
Common Use Passenger Processing Systems: an industry standard that lets many airlines share the same check-in, boarding and kiosk hardware and software, instead of each airline installing its own.
What does Apollo do with SITA technology?
Apollo designs the layout, installs and commissions the equipment, integrates it with DCS, AODB and airline systems, trains staff and provides ongoing support.
Is SITA only for large airports?
No. SITA serves more than 4,900 customers across more than 200 countries and territories, and its technology is used at 95% of airports, according to SITA.
