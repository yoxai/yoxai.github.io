Since 1993, building the systems others depend on.

Apollo was founded in Islamabad in 1993. We started in data networks, grew into contact centres and unified communications for operators and government, and today deliver aviation, telecom and AI platforms with partners from around the world.

Our story

From the first ISP network to AI in production.

  1. 1993

    Founded in Islamabad

    Apollo starts as a systems integrator in Pakistan’s capital.

    Source: apollo.com.pk

  2. 1990s

    Networks with Nortel

    Apollo grows as a Nortel Networks partner into enterprise networks and unified communications.

  3. 1995

    PTCL’s first public data network for an ISP

    Apollo was the technology provider to PTCL for the first public data network of an internet service provider, contracted in 1994 and live in 1995.

    Source: Wikipedia: Shujaat Azeem

  4. 2000s

    Contact centres and unified communications

    IVR, CTI, contact-centre and collaboration systems for operators, banks and government.

  5. 2020s

    Customer-care platforms at national scale

    Customer-care systems for Ufone and PTCL, and a Huawei IVR for the Directorate General of Immigration & Passports.

  6. 2020s

    Apollo’s own platforms

    Sector Fusion, InsightX, the Northern Technik operations platform and PharmaCloud ERP move into production.

    Source: apollo.com.pk

  7. Today

    Aviation and telecom partners

    SITA, ATERMES, SpideRadio and Radio Innovation, across six industries and more than 20 OEM partners.

    Source: apollo.com.pk
