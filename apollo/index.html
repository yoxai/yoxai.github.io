FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Apollo do?
Apollo is a systems integrator founded in Islamabad in 1993. It designs, integrates and supports critical systems for airports, mobile operators, government and enterprises, across six industries.
Which technology partners does Apollo work with?
In aviation, SITA and ATERMES. In telecom, SpideRadio and Radio Innovation. Apollo works with more than 20 OEM partners in total and builds its own platforms, including InsightX and Sector Fusion.
Does Apollo build its own software?
Yes. Apollo has 8 production-ready platforms, including InsightX for AI video, Sector Fusion for network audits, the Northern Technik aviation operations platform and PharmaCloud ERP.
Where is Apollo based?
Apollo’s head office is at 5th Floor, Royal Plaza, 30 East, Blue Area, Islamabad, Pakistan.
How do I start a project with Apollo?
Use the contact page or email info@apollo.com.pk. Tell us what you need to run, connect or protect, and an engineer will reply.
