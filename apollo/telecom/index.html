FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Apollo do for mobile operators?
Radio access and antennas, network audits and RF optimisation, traffic and security management, revenue technology, an eSIM platform and multi-vendor OSS/BSS integration with a 24/7 NOC.
Which telecom partners does Apollo work with?
SpideRadio for 2G, 4G and 5G radio access and Radio Innovation for high-gain antennas. Apollo also builds its own audit platform, Sector Fusion.
Why audit before buying new equipment?
Many coverage and capacity problems come from swapped sectors, wrong azimuths or overshooting cells. Fixing those first is cheaper than adding sites.
