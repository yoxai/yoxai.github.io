FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do Radio Innovation antennas do?
They are high-gain antennas for low, mid and high bands that extend coverage and add capacity from existing sites. Radio Innovation reports up to 400% more rural coverage and over 3x better urban indoor coverage.
Which frequency bands are covered?
Low band 694–960 MHz, mid band 1710–2170 MHz and high band 2300–2690 MHz.
Who uses Radio Innovation?
Radio Innovation lists operators including Airtel, Jio, Orange, Telefónica, Mobily and Digicel on its website.
