Telecom · Apollo platform

Find weak cells from the data, not the drive test.

Sector Fusion is Apollo’s automated network audit platform. It reads measurement reports from the live network, finds sector swaps, azimuth errors and coverage holes, and exports the fixes, cutting physical drive testing by up to 90%.

Workflow

Four steps from raw files to fixes.

  1. Step 01

    Upload

    Load MR and EP measurement files from the live network.

  2. Step 02

    Map and validate

    Data is placed on the map and checked against site and cell data.

  3. Step 03

    Analyse

    Coverage, overshooting, sector-swap, azimuth and neighbour modules run automatically.

  4. Step 04

    Act

    Visual results and exportable optimisation recommendations.

Analysis modules

What Sector Fusion checks.

01

Centralised MR and EP analysis

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What data does Sector Fusion need?
MR (measurement report) and EP measurement files from the live network, plus site and cell data for validation.
Which networks does it work with?
Multi-vendor radio access networks on 2G, 4G and 5G.
How much drive testing can it replace?
Apollo’s figure is up to 90% less physical drive testing, because problems are found from network data instead of field measurements.
