FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an integrated small cell?
A compact base station that combines the baseband unit, radio unit and antenna in one device. SpideRadio’s 5G integrated small cell connects over existing fibre or network cable and is built for indoor and blind-spot coverage.
Which technologies does SpideRadio support?
GSM (2G), LTE (4G) and NR (5G) radio access, a converged 4G/5G core with IMS, 2G/4G/5G network management, and 4G/5G CPE.
Can Apollo integrate SpideRadio with our existing network?
Apollo’s integration practice covers multi-vendor networks and OSS/BSS, and each rollout includes integration and acceptance testing on the live network.
